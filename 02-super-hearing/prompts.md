# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
Outside of the formal interviews have we received any feedback from handlers, Supply, superheroes, or other key stakeholders as to what is working or not working?

### 2.
Based on the interviews alone, group together a quantifiable assessment of what is working, what is not working, and what needs to be fixed in the next release.

### 3.
For the counts of what is working or not working, is there any information from the interviews that points to the contrary? For example, only 1 person is captured as stating filing failure reports is quick - did anyone state they were having issues?

### 4.
Read everything in 00-rook/feedback/tickets/. Same treatment as before: group them, tell me how many are in each group, and quote one line from each.

### 5.
Create a side by side comparison calling out any conflicting information from each reporter (handler, responder, Supply, etc.) between interviews, support tickets, and any additional information that could result in an incorrect priority or scope of work.

### 6.
Is there anywhere in the support ticket data that provides any information about the week of September 7?

### 7.
What are badge size requests?

### 8.
how many responders were on leave the week of September 7? How many were authorized vs. not?

### 9.
Draft the questions for Nadia and Ravi

### 10.
Only look at what came in after 12 August. What's different about these compared to everything before?

### 11.
Re-review the 147 support tickets and provide a summary of common theme /issue, count of tickets, and 2-3 sentences that best describe the issue and and before /after 4.2 mentions.

### 12.
Let's circle back and revisit: You've now read both folders. Where do they disagree? What's loud in the interviews but rare in the tickets, and what's all over the tickets that nobody brought up in the interviews?

### 13.
Is there any public data available to help understand or support the ping data? Was there anything outside the control of our system or people such as a power outage, service interruptions, down database or servers, or other technical failures?

### 14.
Were responders in compromised situations where that may have forced them to go quiet? For example, are there any responders whose personal identities are secret and had they answered a ping in the allotted time indicated they'd give away their superhero identity?

### 15.
Summarize the information from my last three prompts above and draft any recommended emails /questions to the appropriate people to get additional information or clarification.

### 16.
No emails have been sent yet, so you can modify any existing drafts for people as needed

### 17.
Based on the information between the support tickets and interviews, re-summarize the top issues identified that were likely caused by the 4.2 release and what the recommended solutions are

### 18.
Are you able to write a simple process flow from when a call is received to how a responder typically is assigned or picks up the the call and completes it?

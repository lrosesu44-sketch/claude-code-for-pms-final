# Rook Dispatch: where the sources agree, disagree, or could send us the wrong way

Compiled 6 Oct 2026 from: the four September handler interviews, the 147 support tickets (29 Jun to 7 Sep), the ping and responder tables (to 6 Sep), the 4.2 release page comments, the release notes, the product briefs and Q3 roadmap, and the handover as summarised in CLAUDE.md.

Labels: **[verified]** = checked against the database or a page this session. **[inference]** = my reading, not tested. **[handover]** = Priya's opinion, not verified.

## 3. Smaller inconsistencies to know about

- **Ambrose's incident date:** his ticket (13 Aug) says "yesterday evening," so 12 Aug, release day. In the 2 Sep interview he said "two weeks ago" (about 19 Aug). Could be two incidents or loose recall.
- **"3x normal" (Nadia, 18 Aug):** I can't reproduce it from the table. Almost no callout-type tickets exist before 12 Aug, so her ratio depends on her definition. Ask her.
- **Non-callout tickets also doubled** after 4.2 (about 5-8 a week before, 13-17 after), mostly filters and repeated text. Part of the rise may be handlers writing in more generally.
- **Halloran's "steady" responder:** Sgt. Bulwark's pings are up, but his missed rate went from 0% to 18%, among the highest of the non-starved. Not salient to Halloran, but real.
- **Ticket repetition:** only 96 distinct subject lines in 147 tickets, many with identical text across handlers. Treat counts as rough.
